# Tax Machina

A free, offline South African income tax calculator and optimiser for individuals, designed by Kgori Mereki.

Enter your income streams, medical aid dependants and what you already contribute. Tax Machina estimates your normal tax for 2026/27 (2025/26 also available) with full workings, then tests legal ways to pay less: retirement annuity top-ups (s11F), s18A donations, tax-free savings (s12T), interest and CGT exemptions, and medical credits (s6A/s6B). Each lever cites the relevant section of the Income Tax Act, VAT Act or Tax Administration Act.

Single HTML file, no dependencies, no data leaves your browser.

Parameters verified against the SARS Budget 2026 Tax Guide. This is a planning estimate, not tax advice.
